## Why You Need This
Your trading system (live scanners and data collection) currently relies on your laptop being open and running at specific times (9:15 AM - 3:30 PM). 

Setting up a cloud server ensures your system runs 24/7, even when your laptop is fully shut down.

## Your Hosting Options

### 1. Oracle Cloud "Always Free" 
*   **Cost:** ₹0 Forever
*   **Pros:** Very powerful (Up to 4 CPU / 24GB RAM). Perfect for this.
*   **Cons:** **Requires a Visa or Mastercard debit/credit card** with international transactions enabled for a 5-minute verification process. (RuPay is rejected).

### 2. Cheap Indian VPS ( UPI / RuPay Accepted )
*   **Cost:** ~₹300 - ₹500 / month
*   **Providers:** Hostinger India, MilesWeb, BigRock, HostGator India.
*   **Pros:** Very reliable. Extremely easy to buy using UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe) or RuPay cards. No international card verification hurdles. 

### 3. The Hardware Route (Android Termux)
*   **Cost:** ₹0
*   **Pros:** If you have an old spare Android phone, you can install the "Termux" app (a Linux terminal), install Python, and run your scripts on the phone connected to your home WiFi. 
*   **Cons:** Relies on your home internet and phone battery/health.
